Zealate: write books chapter by chapter, read them like books

Zealate is a free place to write a book and read it like a real book. Pages are cut to trade-paperback proportions, two side by side on a wide screen, with page numbers that run through the whole book.

What you can do

  • Write chapter by chapter, in any order, with every word saved as you type.
  • Keep a book private, then publish it chapter by chapter when you are ready.
  • Sign it with your real name, a pen name, or stay anonymous.
  • Write in any language: Hebrew and Arabic read right to left, page one on the right.
  • Export the whole book to Word or PDF at any time.
  • Find and read public books by title, chapter or author.

Questions and answers

Is Zealate free?

Yes. Writing, publishing and reading on Zealate are free.

Who can read my book?

Nobody until you make it public, and then only the chapters you publish.

Which languages can I write in?

Any. The site itself is available in Hebrew, English, Arabic, German, Spanish and French.
